How saving works
  • Save with the Save/Quit button on the in-game toolbar, then pick one of three slots. You can save while walking around town, but not inside a place or during a conversation or scene. Picking a filled slot asks you to click it again before replacing it.
  • Autosave happens every morning when you wake up, in its own slot.
  • Load from the title screen. You get the same slot list, including the autosave.
  • Saves stay in this browser on this computer. Use Export saves for a backup file and Import saves to bring one back (this replaces the saves here and restarts the game).
  • Click the game once to give it keyboard focus before typing your name.
