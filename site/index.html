      How saving works
      • Save from the toolbar's Save/Quit button while walking around town. You can't save inside a place, during a conversation or during a scene.
      • Autosave happens every in-game morning, in its own slot.
      • Load from the title screen.
      • Full screen: the button under the game. Press Esc, or the button again, to leave.
      • Settings (top right): volume, pausing when you switch away, graphics quality, backup reminders and more.
      • Saves are stored in this browser on this device. They aren't uploaded anywhere. Download a backup now and then, or link a save folder, so clearing your browser data can't erase them.
      Playing on a Steam Deck
      • Open this page in Chrome or Firefox: in Desktop Mode, or in Game Mode after adding the browser to Steam. The touchscreen and trackpads work as the mouse.
      • Saves stay in that browser on the Deck. In Chrome you can link a save folder, for example in /home/deck/Documents.
      • For full controller support, an on-screen keyboard and no browser, use the native Steam Deck build. Your backups from this page import into it.
